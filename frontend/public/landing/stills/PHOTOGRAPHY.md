# Landing photography

The supporting images are real photographs, hosted locally for privacy and reliable loading. They are illustrative photography, not Medcore employees, facilities, customers, or endorsements.

- `clinical-conversation.webp`: National Cancer Institute, a doctor consulting with a patient. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/doctor-talking-to-hospital-patient-DK--4VWK1tw).
- `hospital-lobby.webp`: National Cancer Institute, NIH Building 10 lobby entrance. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/white-and-green-glass-building-P1IhmLjxW5M).
- Both downloaded September 21, 2026 under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Resized and encoded as WebP by the source image service.
- `presence.webp` remains generated editorial imagery in the opening hero. It is not a photograph of an actual customer facility or team.
- `workspace.webp` is the actual application with synthetic demonstration data.

- `product-doctors.png` and `product-reports.png` are unchanged application captures from `docs/screenshots/`, showing demonstration data. The overview preview focuses on the capacity and admissions panels, rather than the generated decorative banner.
